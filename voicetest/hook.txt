Costco sells you a hot dog and a soda for a dollar fifty. Same price since 1985.
Years ago, an executive asked the founder if they could finally raise it. His answer? "If you raise the hot dog, I will kill you. Figure it out."
And here's the crazy part. Costco loses money on that hot dog. On purpose.
So how does a store that refuses to make money on what it sells become one of the most valuable retailers on the planet?
The answer is a little plastic card in your wallet.
